Cristiano Ronaldo's Journey After Real Madrid

Juventus (2018 - 2021)

Cristiano Ronaldo signed with Juventus in July 2018, marking the start of a new chapter in his illustrious career. During his tenure at Juventus, he won two Serie A titles and was pivotal in many crucial Champions League matches.

Manchester United (2021 - 2022)

Ronaldo returned to Manchester United in August 2021, rejoining the club where he made a significant mark earlier in his career. He continued to showcase his skills and scored memorable goals, including his dramatic late-game headers.

Al Nassr (2023 - Present)

In December 2022, Ronaldo took another significant step in his career by joining Al Nassr, a Saudi Arabian club. His presence has elevated the team's profile and continues to attract global attention to the league.
